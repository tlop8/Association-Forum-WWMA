    About the data

    Fictional dashboard as of November 13, 2026. The 500 survey profiles cover Q3 and Q4 2026. Replay adds the last 50 profiles and their linked activity. Historical program totals are simulated separately.

    Capacity counts all available registration spots, including member and non-member spots. Actual attendance counts check-ins for completed sessions. Net registration revenue counts payments less refunds, including paid registrations for upcoming events. Mix charts use their own year and follow the quarter, format, and program filters. Membership figures count active members at the latest point in the selected quarter and renewals among members due to renew. Research grants use the full 2026 budget.
